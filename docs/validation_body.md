# Whole-body engine validation: midazolam

Generated by `scripts/validate_body.py`. Observed data: plasma concentration–time profiles from 28 IV and 54 oral clinical datasets (aggregated means and typical subjects), digitized by the Open Systems Pharmacology project from the original publications (see `data/clinical/`).

## Scenarios

- **Bottom-up**: no clinical data used. Liver clearance is scaled from in vitro microsomal kinetics (CYP3A4 Vmax 850 pmol/min/mg, Km 4 µM; UGT1A4 276 pmol/min/mg, Km 37.8 µM; 32 mg microsomal protein/g liver). Tissue distribution comes from physicochemistry (Rodgers & Rowland). Gut-wall first pass from the Qgut model.
- **Middle-out**: one multiplier on intrinsic clearance (×1.13) fitted to the IV datasets only, then the oral datasets are predicted blind. Under-prediction of clearance by microsomal IVIVE is a well-known systematic effect.

Reference male, bottom-up: midazolam in ICRP 89 reference male: Vss 64 L (0.88 L/kg), B:P 0.60, fu 0.031, CLint,hep 760 L/h -> CL_plasma 17.1 L/h (hepatic blood 28.2, renal 0.24), t1/2,terminal ~4.1 h (effective ln2*Vss/CL ~2.6 h) | oral: ka 1.65/h, Fa 0.98, Fg 0.49, Fh 0.72

Reference male, middle-out: midazolam in ICRP 89 reference male: Vss 64 L (0.88 L/kg), B:P 0.60, fu 0.031, CLint,hep 856 L/h -> CL_plasma 18.6 L/h (hepatic blood 30.7, renal 0.24), t1/2,terminal ~3.9 h (effective ln2*Vss/CL ~2.4 h) | oral: ka 1.65/h, Fa 0.98, Fg 0.46, Fh 0.69

| bottom-up | PO | AUC | 54 | 50% | 81% | 1.57 | 1.47 |
| bottom-up | PO | CMAX | 54 | 61% | 93% | 1.43 | 1.29 |
| middle-out | IV | AUC | 28 | 68% | 86% | 1.45 | 1.22 |

GMFE = geometric mean fold error (1.0 = perfect). The 2-fold criterion is the conventional acceptance bar for PBPK predictions.

## Known limitations (v0.1)

- IV doses are modeled as 2-minute infusions (actual durations were not recorded in the source data).
- Subjects are the ICRP reference male unless the dataset gives sex/weight/age (Greenblatt 1984).
- Linear kinetics (concentrations far below Km at these doses). Fed-state effects are not modeled.
- Intestinal permeability is an assumed value (BCS class I); it sets absorption rate and gut-wall extraction.
